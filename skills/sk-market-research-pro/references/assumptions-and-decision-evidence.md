# Market research assumptions and decision evidence

| Area | Expected evidence |
|---|---|
| Decision | Go/no-go, segment priority, pricing, expansion or explicit exploratory status |
| Boundary | Category definition, geography, customer segment, time horizon and exclusions |
| Sizing | TAM/SAM/SOM formula, denominator, unit, source date and sensitivity range |
| Competition | Direct, substitute and adjacent alternatives with comparable criteria |
| Signals | Source ID, metric definition, period, quality and whether observed or inferred |
| Recommendation | Option, confidence, rationale, why-not alternatives and trigger to revisit |
| Risk | Market uncertainty, stale data, double counting, survivorship bias and mitigation |

Never present a precise market number without boundary and denominator logic. A decision-ready output shows assumptions and sensitivity, not only a polished chart or a list of competitors.
