Problem Statement

Testers cannot efficiently review BA artifacts because they are written in markdown format that is not visual or accessible to non-technical stakeholders.

The current workflow requires testers to parse complex markdown tables and mermaid diagrams manually, leading to missed requirements and inconsistent test coverage.
